\chapter{Relation with a Real-World Problem}\label{ch:realworld}

\begin{draftnote}
MODULE 6 --- KUET-specific chapter, easy to forget, no equivalent in a generic ML thesis.

The accepted book (\texttt{kuet\_thesis\_style/Thesis\_book\_draft\_3.pdf}, Chapter 5) runs about
two pages with no sub-sections, and moves through four beats: industrial relevance, the
engineering contribution, the socio-economic argument, then an explicit mapping onto named
United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. It names SDG 4, 8, 9 and 12.

For this thesis the honest line is that a manipulator which can be given a stated safety budget,
rather than a hand-tuned penalty weight, is easier to certify and to hand to a non-specialist
operator --- and that the seed-variance finding in Chapter~\ref{ch:results} is a reliability
result, which is what an industrial deployment actually cares about. Do not overclaim: nothing
here ran on hardware.
\end{draftnote}
