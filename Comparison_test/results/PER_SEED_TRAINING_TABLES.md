# Per-seed training results — matrix-v2, 3-arm batch

Generated by `results/scripts/make_per_seed_tables.py` from `results/tb_csv/`.
Source runs: commit `567e4c0`, tag `matrix-v2`, task `Isaac-Lift-Cube-UR5e-v0`, 1500 iterations at `num_envs = 4096`, `rsl_rl` 3.0.1.

**Three values per arm.** `final` is the value at the last logged iteration. `mean` is the arithmetic mean over all 1501 logged points — it includes the untrained early phase, so it measures learning-curve area rather than converged performance. `tail` is the mean over the final 10% of iterations, which is the statistic used in `MATRIX_V2_PARTIAL_3ARM.md` and in Chapter 4 — **quote `tail`, not `mean`, when comparing against those documents.**

**† Mean episode cost for `ppo`.** `Loss/mean_episode_cost` is written only by the Lagrangian runner, so the `ppo` arm never logged it. The value shown is `ctrl`'s, which is valid because `ppo` and `ctrl` were shown to be bitwise identical — all 68 actor and reward-critic tensors byte-for-byte equal, on all ten seeds, reconfirmed independently at evaluation time (`MATRIX_V2_PARTIAL_3ARM.md` §2). `ctrl` exists precisely to measure the episodic cost of a policy that does not act on it.

**Violation rows** are soft-margin step fractions from training telemetry, i.e. the fraction of control steps on which a continuous margin fell the wrong side of a threshold, measured on a still-exploring policy. They are *not* the frozen-policy safety numbers reported in Chapter 4 §4.5, which count true singularity crossings (w < 1e-4) and per-episode joint-limit contact over 30,000 evaluation episodes. Do not substitute one for the other.
